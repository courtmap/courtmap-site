Inside the app

Every court. One app.

Features

Less group-chat chaos.
More time on court.

Everything between “anyone up for a game?” and the first serve, in one app.

A new challenge every month.

Each month brings a fresh challenge to play for. Every game you play earns points toward it, so you climb the leaderboard and finish with that month's badge.

  • Twelve themed challenges a year
  • A leaderboard for every challenge
  • A badge for each one you finish

Search courts the way you actually decide.

Filter by distance, time and your preferences, then sort what's left. Every court shows the sport, how far it is and what players rated it.

  • Distance, time and preference filters
  • Ratings and reviews from players
  • Save and favourite the courts you love

Every court around you, on one map.

Start from your blue dot and see every court light up around it. Tap a pin for distance and rating, then join without leaving the map.

  • Clustered pins show how many courts are nearby
  • Swipe through court cards right on the map
  • Jump from a pin to Join in one tap

Open games, filled.

Pick a day and see games with open spots. You can see who's already in before you hit Join, and every game you play counts toward your points.

  • Week view of open games near you
  • See who's in and how many spots are left
  • Earn points for every game you play

Your clubs, in one feed.

Join active groups, clubs and venues in your area. Ask which court to try, find a fourth for doubles, and upvote the posts worth seeing.

  • Group chat and feed for each club
  • Sort by most recent or most popular
  • Comment, save and upvote posts

Meetups worth showing up for.

Every group's upcoming events in one list, with where they are and what they cost up front. When it's over, it moves to Past events.

  • Upcoming and past events for each group
  • Location and price on every event
  • From casual hits to organized meets

FAQ

Questions,
answered

Something else on your mind? Join the waitlist and we'll keep you posted.

Is CourtMap free?

Yes. Creating an account and joining CourtMap is free. Some events run by groups and venues may have their own fee, which is always shown up front.

Which sports does CourtMap support?

Tennis, padel and pickleball to start. Every court is tagged with its sport, so you can filter for exactly what you want to play.

Where is CourtMap available?

We're starting in Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area, including Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham and Oakville. More cities are on the way.

How do points and badges work?

Games and challenges earn you points. Points move you up the monthly leaderboards and unlock badges that stay on your profile.

Can I find people at my level?

Use the Preferences filter to narrow games to what suits you, and see who's already in a game before you join.

When can I start using it?

We're opening access in waves. Join the waitlist with your email and we'll send your invite as soon as your spot is ready.
